01 · How it works

What bank credit portfolios are and how recovery works.

When repayment of a loan stops over time, for the bank that loan becomes an asset that absorbs capital. For a specialised investor it is a stream of future collections, bought today at a discount.

01

Sale

European banks and credit institutions periodically sell portfolios of banking and consumer credit, made up of thousands of small positions.

02

Purchase at a discount

The buyer pays a fraction of face value, depending on the age of the claim, its documentation and the estimated probability of recovery.

03

Specialised recovery

Authorised servicers manage out-of-court and judicial recovery, within the framework of Directive (EU) 2021/2167 on credit servicers and purchasers.

04

Progressive collections

Collections accrue over time. Many small positions are settled through negotiated repayment plans, faster than court proceedings.

GranularRisk spread across thousands of positions, borrowers and regions.
UncorrelatedCollections depend on recovery, not on equity and bond markets.
RecurringSales repeat every quarter: the market regenerates with new portfolios.
Why private credit is replacing banks

European prudential rules penalise credit that has stopped performing on bank balance sheets: the longer it stays, the more capital it absorbs. Selling at a discount is often more efficient than waiting years for recovery.

The space is taken by non-bank investors: private credit funds, securitisation vehicles and specialised operators, which buy the portfolios and organise recovery through authorised servicers. It is an industrial activity, built on data, pricing and operational discipline.

Sources: Regulation (EU) 2019/630 (calendar provisioning); Directive (EU) 2021/2167 on credit servicers and credit purchasers.

02 · Insights

Five questions to ask before allocating to bank credit.

They apply to any vehicle. The answers separate a structured operation from a bet.

Recovery is the core of the operation. In Europe the activity is governed by Directive (EU) 2021/2167, transposed by each Member State with servicer authorisation regimes. A structured vehicle names its servicer, the authorisation it operates under and how often it reports recovery data.

The price must be read together with expected recovery, never alone. Ask how the estimate is built: on which data, with which method, with how much prudence. An offer calculated on the minimum expected recovery is more defensible than one built on the average.

The recovery cycle of granular portfolios typically lasts more than a year. It matters whether proceeds are distributed during the cycle or only at the end, and whether collections are reinvested in new portfolios.

In an institutional structure the functions are separate: an authorised manager responsible for the investment policy, a depositary bank holding the capital, an independent auditor certifying accounts and valuations, a vehicle whose assets are segregated by law.

Recovery below expectations, court timelines longer than planned, illiquidity, servicer operational risk, regulatory and tax risk, possible total loss of capital. The offering documents of each vehicle describe them in detail and are the only basis for a decision.

03 · The opportunity

Why bank credit portfolios are a structural opportunity.

This is not a passing cycle. European banks have a permanent regulatory incentive to sell credit that stops performing, and a market of specialised buyers has organised itself to receive it. Three numbers show the scale.

A recurring flow, not a one-off

European calendar provisioning requires banks to fully cover non-performing credit within a few years. Holding it costs capital every quarter: selling is an industrial decision, repeated every financial year.

Return comes from the discount

The buyer pays a fraction of face value and collects what it manages to recover. The outcome depends on the ratio between price paid and recovery achieved, not on the performance of a listed market.

Cash flows uncorrelated with markets

Collections come from repayment plans and recoveries across thousands of small positions. They do not follow rates, equity indices or spreads: they are a distinct source of cash flows compared with a traditional portfolio.

A market that has matured

Authorised and supervised servicers, a secondary market worth almost half of all trades, historical recovery data on which to build pricing. Today the asset class is analysed with method, not bet on.

What it means for an allocator

For a family office or a professional investor, bank credit portfolios are a private credit component with three features rarely found together: discounted access to an asset banks have to sell, progressive cash flows along the recovery cycle, low correlation with the rest of the portfolio.

The other side deserves the same attention: the investment is illiquid for the length of the cycle, recovery may be lower or slower than expected and the quality of the servicer is decisive. This is why access is reserved for investors able to assess these risks, and why the questions in the previous section matter more than any promise.
